# Incident Response Plan

Status: readiness template; not yet approved, staffed, exercised, or operating.

1. A named incident commander receives an alert or report, opens a restricted incident record, assigns severity, preserves time-synchronized evidence, and records the decision basis.
2. Contain with the smallest reversible action: revoke affected sessions/keys, isolate the affected product installation, or disable the compromised integration. Do not erase evidence or broaden outages unnecessarily.
3. Determine affected products, customer installations, people/data, time window, root access path, integrity impact, and whether privacy, legal, contractual, provider, insurance, or law-enforcement notification duties apply.
4. Eradicate and recover from a reviewed exact candidate and protected backup. Reapply privacy deletion ledgers before restored systems serve traffic. Verify schema, isolation, secrets, authorization, health, logs, and affected workflows.
5. Only the owner-designated communication authority approves customer, regulator, provider, or public messages. Agents may prepare facts but may not decide legal notification or send external notices without exact approval.
6. Within the approved deadline, complete a blameless review with timeline, cause, impact, detection/response metrics, evidence links, corrective owners/dates, and closure verification.

Required before an audit period: named roles and backups, severity/notification matrix, secure reporting route, contact tree, evidence store, provider escalation paths, legal review, annual tabletop, and tracked corrective actions.
