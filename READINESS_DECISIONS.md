# Readiness Decisions Required From The Owner

These decisions cannot be invented by code and are required before formal SOC 2 readiness review or Production launch.

1. Name the security/compliance lead and owner for every control.
2. Approve the system boundary, Production environments, and excluded systems.
3. Approve availability objective, recovery-time objective (RTO), and recovery-point objective (RPO).
4. Select identity/MFA, hosting, secret storage, logging/monitoring, backup, payment, email, and DNS providers.
5. Approve vulnerability remediation deadlines and exception authority.
6. Approve access-review cadence, emergency-access process, and offboarding deadline.
7. Approve incident severity definitions, notification authority, and tabletop cadence.
8. Approve evidence storage and retention with the selected CPA firm.
9. Obtain legal review of privacy notice, customer terms, retention, legal holds, subprocessors, and player opt-out language.
10. Select an independent licensed CPA firm and confirm Trust Services scope and observation expectations.
